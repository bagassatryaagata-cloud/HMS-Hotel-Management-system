Key features

What each feature gives your team.

01Key feature

Asset Management

Register assets, track where they are, and follow their movement, repair, and disposal.

  • Know where every asset isEach asset has a registered location and department.
  • Traceable movementTransfers between departments and properties are logged and approved.
  • Repairs stay with the assetEach repair is kept on the asset it belongs to.
  • Controlled disposalScrap, sale, or destruction goes through approval before the asset leaves the register.
02Key feature

Maintenance & Work Orders

From a staff report to a confirmed fix, each step of a maintenance request stays on record.

  • Report in secondsStaff submit a request with room, category, and priority.
  • Supervisors review firstRequests are approved before engineering picks them up.
  • One queue for engineeringTechnicians see assigned orders and update status as they work.
  • Closed only when confirmedEngineering confirms the Resolved status after the job is done.
03Key feature

Preventive Maintenance

Plan routine upkeep with templates and schedules, and keep every inspection on record.

  • Reusable templatesBuild a PM template once and use it for schedules.
  • Planned, not guessedPM schedules set when each routine check is due.
  • Tasks you can followPM tasks show what is assigned, in progress, or done.
  • Inspection historyCompleted tasks are kept in PM history for later review.
04

Operations Dashboard

A summary of asset inventory, open work, repairs, and pending PM, in one view.

  • Inventory at a glanceSee how many registered units are operational or need attention.
  • Open work in viewActive work orders and repairs show up without opening each module.
  • Upcoming PMPending inspections appear on the same screen.
  • Recent activityThe latest work orders, PM tasks, and movements in one log.
05Key feature

Asset Registration

Record asset details, categories, and locations in one place.

  • Organized Asset InformationKeep asset details organized in one place.
  • Easy Asset ReferenceFind recorded asset information when needed.
06Key feature

Archive Center

Keep archived records organized and accessible when needed.

  • Organized RecordsKeep archived information organized for reference.
  • Historical ReferenceReview archived records when needed.
07Key feature

Master Data Management

Manage the reference data used across asset and maintenance records.

  • Consistent InformationMaintain consistent reference data across records.
  • Centralized DataManage reference data in one place.
08Key feature

Asset Repair

Record asset repairs and keep track of their maintenance status.

  • Repair Status OverviewReview the current status of asset repairs.
  • Repair InformationAccess recorded details about asset repairs.
